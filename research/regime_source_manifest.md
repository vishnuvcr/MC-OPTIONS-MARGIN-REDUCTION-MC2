## Cross-market

BSE Sensex is used as a domestic cross-market regime label. Global equity/volatility and gold/FX are secondary stress labels when historical observations are available without look-ahead.

These fields are joined by date and are not permitted to alter the locked 09:30 BATMAN signal.

## Research use

Regime variables are used to answer:
- Does capital efficiency vary across volatility regimes?
- Does the candidate survive high-volatility/tail regimes?
- Are apparent improvements concentrated in particular FII/DII flow conditions?
- Do global overnight stress conditions explain or destabilise the observed difference?

The final analysis reports unconditional performance first, followed by regime-stratified results.
