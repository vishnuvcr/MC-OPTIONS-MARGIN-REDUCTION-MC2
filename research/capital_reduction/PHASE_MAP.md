# Capital-Reduction Track — Phase Map

| Phase | Branch | Main question | Required output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | capital-reduction-phase-1 | What exactly is the capital/margin metric, what parameter families can change it, and what data are required? | Literature matrix, margin protocol, parameter registry, data-source matrix |
| 2 | capital-reduction-phase-2 | What is the locked baseline's actual capital requirement by expiry? | Baseline capital ledger with historical margin/premium/cost decomposition |
| 3 | capital-reduction-phase-3 | Which pre-registered BATMAN parameters reduce capital most efficiently? | Candidate frontier and capital-reduction ranking |
| 4 | capital-reduction-phase-4 | Does the capital reduction survive unseen data and stress? | Walk-forward, paired inference, regime and cost/liquidity stress |
| 5 | capital-reduction-phase-5 | What is the final combined scientific conclusion? | Combined manuscript, figures, tables, appendices, supplement |

## Branching rule
Each phase starts from the completed prior phase branch. The previous Sensex-integrated research branches remain untouched.

## Combined manuscript
The final Phase 5 manuscript will integrate:
1. the original MC2 BATMAN research;
2. the capital-reduction parameter research;
3. Sensex cross-market diagnostics;
4. margin methodology and capital decomposition;
5. the final evidence for or against a parameter-driven capital reduction.
